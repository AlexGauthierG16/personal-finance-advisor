Spend Analyzer, for students

Upload your bank statement, set your monthly budget, and see where your money goes. All amounts are in EUR.

1. Your statement

Columns: date (YYYY-MM-DD), description, amount (negative = money out). Descriptions and amounts are sent to an AI model (OpenAI) to be categorised.

2. Your monthly budget

Starting values for a student living in Milan. Change them to match your life.

3. Spending against budget

Variance = actual − budget. Positive (red) means over budget.
